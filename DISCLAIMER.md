## English

Its “fortune” is an interpretive product output. It is not a factual prediction of future events, a mathematically proven fortune, a scientific forecast, a divine revelation, or a guaranteed prophecy. Do not substitute it for professional medical, legal, financial, or safety advice or for your own assessment of actual conditions.

This project is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or representative of any religious institution or faith community. References to religious traditions are used for cultural, philosophical, literary, and interpretive purposes. They do not constitute religious instruction, proselytization, or authoritative theology. Wanxiangli neither seeks to persuade anyone to adopt a faith nor claims to reconcile or unify distinct traditions.

Its Buddhist, philosophical and religious Daoist, and Christian notes are limited readings based on sources listed in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md). Each tradition contains substantial internal diversity. No note should be treated as the only correct interpretation, a statement for every school or denomination, a replacement for clergy or qualified religious advisers, or a resolution of theological disputes. The project does not impersonate religious figures or attribute invented words to them.

Islamic traditions are intentionally outside the content scope of this project. This is a product-scope decision intended to avoid inaccurate representation or unintended offense, and does not express any judgment regarding Islam, Muslims, or related cultures.

We aim to cite sources, preserve context, and discuss traditions and their adherents respectfully. Harassment, degradation, dehumanization, or hostility based on religion is prohibited under the [religious content policy](RELIGIOUS_CONTENT_POLICY.md) and [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). That commitment is part of contribution review and output rules, not a claim that a disclaimer alone prevents mistakes.
