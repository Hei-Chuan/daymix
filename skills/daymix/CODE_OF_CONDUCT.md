# Code of Conduct / 行为准则

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free space for contributors and users. This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, and project discussions.

Disagreement about code, sources, or interpretations is welcome when expressed with care. Harassment, threats, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, and derogatory or dehumanizing remarks are not. Discrimination or hostility based on religion, race, ethnicity, nationality, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, or other personal characteristics is prohibited. Do not disclose another person's private information without consent.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary. For a concern, contact a maintainer through a contact channel published on their GitHub profile, or use GitHub's abuse-reporting tools. Do not post sensitive evidence in a public issue. Reports will be considered on their merits, with attention to the safety of people involved.

This concise policy follows the principles of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/); it is not a claim of affiliation with its authors.
